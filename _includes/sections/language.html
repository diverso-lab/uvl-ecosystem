UVL at a glance

A feature model is a tree. Indentation gives the hierarchy, keywords give the relationship between a parent and its children, and constraints cover everything the tree cannot express.

mandatory

The child must be selected whenever its parent is.

optional

The child may be selected if its parent is.

or

At least one child is selected with the parent.

alternative

Exactly one child is selected with the parent.

constraints

Cross-tree propositional formulas over features, such as A => B.

Language levels

Tools do not need to support everything. UVL is split into stacked levels: a model declares the extensions it uses with include, and tools can convert models down to the level they understand.
