How to contribute

Language

Propose a language change

Write a UVL Enhancement Proposal. See the process below.

 Models

Share your models

Open a pull request on uvl-models, or publish a dataset with a DOI on UVLHub.

 Ecosystem

List your tool

Does your tool read or write UVL? Open an issue and it will appear in the ecosystem.

 Code

Report bugs & send code

Issues and pull requests are welcome on the parsers, the metamodel and the language server.

 Events

Join MODEVAR

Present your work and take part in the design discussions at the workshop.

The UVLEP process

  1. 1Idea

    Open a thread in Discussions.

  2. 2Draft

    Fill in the template and open a pull request.

  3. 3Review

    Maintainers and community discuss and request changes.

  4. 4Decision

    Accepted, rejected or deferred once there is consensus.

  5. 5Implementation

    Accepted proposals land in the grammar and tools.
